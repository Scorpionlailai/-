The v20 generic post-fusion context block improved repair MSE but worsened forecast metrics. This trial tests multiple independent judgments of the **same H/V/T evidence** rather than adding a generic context block.

Keep v12's H/V/T candidate extraction, source encoders, width-64 repair latent, explicit decoder and observed-value clamp, original width-16 PatchTST predictor, masks, and training losses. Compute H/V/T evidence once. Replace the single router by three independent copies of its `none/H/V/T` residual transformations and score networks. Each head produces a repaired latent proposal. For each damaged patch, score each proposal by a zero-initialized learned function of that head's four route weights, route entropy, and observed fraction, plus a fixed penalty equal to its root-mean-square deviation from the three-head mean residual. Softmax across heads and take the weighted residual mean. The consensus starts from the v12 router's residual scale, has an exact base-latent bypass, and uses no future labels. The fixed disagreement penalty has coefficient `1.0`; there is no diversity or auxiliary forecast loss. H retrieval and V/T source extraction are shared, so the extra cost is limited to three router heads and consensus.

Train fresh Stage 1 and Stage 2 for 100 epochs each on locked ETTh1 P4 96→96, seed 2021, batch 128. Stage 1 selects earliest minimum fixed-validation hidden-coordinate repair MSE. Stage 2 uses `MSE + 0.2 MAE`, selecting earliest minimum fixed-validation forecast MAE. Promote only if validation forecast MAE is strictly below `0.5882120694703256` and MSE no higher than `0.7454477286392869`; otherwise retain v12 and do not run v21 on test.
