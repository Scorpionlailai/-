\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
We introduced \Model, a coarse-to-fine multivariate time-series imputer that combines a strong contextual estimate with explicit multi-source patch evidence and observation-conditioned residual refinement. Paired capacity controls identify an accuracy contribution from the full evidence pathway that remains after refinement. A common-refiner study separates backbone quality from second-stage correction. Together with source, reading-granularity and cost analyses, these results characterize how evidence organization and numerical refinement contribute to recovery.
